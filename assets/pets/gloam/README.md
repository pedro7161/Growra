# Gloam

Halloween companion concept: a cute-spooky pumpkin phantom wrapped in a charcoal-purple ghost cloak, with glowing amber eyes and curling violet wisps.

- `base.png`: small pumpkin ghost with autumn leaves and spectral wisps
- `evo1.png`: taller phantom with a witch hat and floating pumpkin embers
- `evo2.png`: grand spectral guardian with a starry cloak and glowing amber emblem

All three are 1254×1254 RGBA sprites with transparent backgrounds, generated in Growra's detailed pixel-art style. The same pumpkin face, purple cloak and warm amber glow carry through all three growth stages.

These are staged art assets. Gloam is not registered in the pet template or companion-unlock systems yet; its Halloween availability rule needs to be set before it can appear in-game.
